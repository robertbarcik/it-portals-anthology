Five Service Desks That Forgot They Were Software.

Five low-fidelity prototypes of an IT services portal. Same prompt, same cheap LLM at the other end of the wire. Five completely different ideas about what a support interface could feel like — none of them a chat box with a purple gradient.

— The Curator's Note

For the past few years every helpful interface has settled into the same shape: a chat input, a list of bubbles, and a cheerful nothingness. This anthology is a small protest against that flattening. Each of the five exhibits is a sketch of an IT services portal — useful, low-fidelity, deliberately committed to one aesthetic and one interaction pattern. None of them are products. All of them work.

Open them in any order. Each one wants your OpenRouter key on first use and forgets it whenever you say.

The exhibit

Five sketches, not products. All five share one OpenRouter-hosted Qwen model on the back end; each prototype hands it a different system prompt to bend its voice to that exhibit's register. No backend, no database, no chat history beyond your session.
